# Space Diner: Google Play listing

**App name** (29/30): Space Diner: Idle Burger Chef

**Short description** (73/80): Grill, serve and grow a burger diner on the Moon, then launch it to Mars!

**Full description** (1548/4000):

```
Run the only burger joint on the Moon.

Grill the food, carry a wobbly stack of it to the counter, ring up the queue of hungry space travelers and grab your cash. Clear the tables, stand on build pads to grow the diner, and when it's fully built, strap it to a rocket and launch to Mars. Then on to Europa.

ZERO-G TOSS
It's low gravity up here, so why walk? Hold TOSS, watch the arc and let go to fling your whole stack across the diner onto the counter. Long throws earn tips, and a bullseye doubles them.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Drag anywhere to move. Pick-ups, drop-offs and the register all work by standing on them
• 19 unlocks per planet: tables, a second grill, new stations, a drone delivery window, a jukebox and a space garden
• Hire three hovering alien staff: a cashier, a runner and a cleaner
• Upgrade your speed, carry capacity, profit and staff
• Quests, VIP guests, tips and rush hours keep every shift busy

THREE PLANETS, NINE FOODS
• Moon Base: burgers, fries and soda
• Mars Outpost: hot dogs, pizza and ice cream
• Europa Station: tacos, donuts and coffee
Every new planet pays 4× more, and after Europa the galaxy loops at even higher stakes.

IDLE FRIENDLY
• Your diner keeps cooking while you're away: collect up to 3 hours of offline earnings
• Progress saves automatically on your phone
• A free 2× cash boost whenever it's ready

MADE FOR PHONES
• Plays offline, no account needed
• Pause any time; the game pauses itself if a call comes in
• Sound and vibration can be turned off in the pause menu

How big can your diner get?
```
