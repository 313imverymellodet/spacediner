## App Review information
**Notes for the reviewer:**
```
Milky Space Diner is a single-player offline game; no account or login is needed.

In-app purchase: "Remove Ads" (remove_ads, non-consumable) is offered on the title screen (NO ADS button) and in the pause menu. Restore Purchases is under Settings on the title screen and in the pause menu (gear icon, top right during play).

Ads: optional rewarded videos only (2X CASH, doubling offline earnings and the daily reward). No ad ever plays without the player tapping a button. Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown once at first launch, after Google's consent form where it applies.

Notifications: the game asks for notification permission only after the player claims their first daily reward, and only schedules local reminders (no push server).
```
**Sign-in required:** No
